# Economy report

Generated by `pnpm economy`: 8 whole campaigns of Setor Ferrugem per difficulty, played by a fixed policy with
the scripted mission routes. Each day the policy treats every infected survivor it has medicine for, builds upgrades
it can afford (oficina, muro, horta, enfermaria), recruits while the squads are short and the shelter has more than four
people, then operates unless the squads carry fewer than 80 rounds between them (then it rests): the siege when
there is one, the railway convoy when at least three people wait, otherwise the richest sector it can reach. The
routes never adapt, so these numbers show where the stock runs dry or piles up; they are not a difficulty rating for
a person.
