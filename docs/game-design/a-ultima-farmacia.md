# Mission design — "A Última Farmácia" on Bairro da Ferrugem

96×96 m, two avenues crossing at the centre (x/y 44–51).

| Area                    | Content                                                                                                                                              | Risk                                      |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| NW yards (start)        | squads spawn in the north yard; houses; **the alley** (x 20–22, walled) runs south to the cross street                                               | 1 walker in the yards                     |
| NE market               | large hall with stalls, doors W/E/S                                                                                                                  | 3 runners inside, 3 walkers around        |
| Crossroads / avenues    | wrecks, rubble; the obvious route                                                                                                                    | 9 walkers + 2 runners on/near the avenues |
| SE pharmacy             | low-fence perimeter, front door (closed), storeroom behind an inner door, **locked back door** to the east alley; medicine cabinet on the north wall | 2 walkers in the yard, 1 inside           |
| SW parking (extraction) | radio beacon and 16×10 m extraction zone                                                                                                             | 1 walker                                  |

Routes:

- **Stealth**: alley → cross west street → lane between workshop and house → service lane → cross south avenue →
  south lane → east alley → force the locked back door (mechanic is fastest) → storeroom (civilian) → cabinet →
  back out south → parking.
- **Combat**: north strip → main avenue south through the crossroads → pharmacy gate and front door → back down the
  avenue → parking. Every shot feeds the four reinforcement entries at the avenue/street ends.
- **Door play**: the back door is the barricade/interaction route; any door can be closed (interact) to hold infected,
  who will bash it and make noise while doing so.

Canonical seed 1337. Infected placement is drawn from zone rectangles with the mission seed.
